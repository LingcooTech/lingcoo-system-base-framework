# Code of Conduct

Lingcoo Frame contributors and maintainers are expected to make participation respectful, safe, and productive.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, specific, and considerate in technical discussion.
- Respect different backgrounds, experience levels, and viewpoints.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, never on a person's identity or motives.
- Accept feedback gracefully and correct mistakes openly.
- Protect private security reports, personal information, and customer data.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, and sustained disruption are
not acceptable in project spaces or when representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to a repository maintainer through their GitHub profile. Do not post sensitive
details in a public issue. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock discussions, reject contributions, or
temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical, with
attention to the safety of the reporter and affected community members.

This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, community events, and other official
project spaces.
